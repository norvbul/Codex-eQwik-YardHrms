# Commercial launch gates

This delivery implements the integrated development application; it does not authorize production launch or certify Jamaican payroll.

The automated foundation/core-HR/payroll/licensing checks, native fresh migrations, secret-free frozen installation, private export controls and encrypted recovery drill are development evidence. Commercial launch additionally requires:

1. An independently reviewed effective-dated Jamaican statutory package and signed reference vectors for every frequency, tax/cap/YTD boundary, proration and employer contribution case. Confirm legal identifier formats, refund treatment and accepted bank layouts.
2. Approved plan prices/currencies/durations, payment evidence requirements, commercial exceptions and separation-of-duties policy. The software defaults to independent verification and disabled commercial exceptions.
3. Approved hosting/region/data residency, real HTTPS ingress/database/Redis/SMTP/S3, encrypted volumes/buckets and private network IAM. Configure a real up-to-date ClamAV service before releasing uploaded documents/evidence.
4. Managed encrypted daily backups/PITR, S3 version recovery, protected key custody, approved retention/legal holds and measured RPO/RTO with scheduled restoration evidence.
5. Owner-approved customer licence, privacy/data-processing notices, trademark/disclaimer copy, employment/payroll obligations and lost-MFA recovery/support policy.
6. Representative legacy exports and source-specific financial/leave/licensing mappings; reconciled historical totals/documents, a parallel full pay cycle and signed cutover/rollback acceptance.
7. Independent OWASP/tenant-isolation penetration testing, realistic load/P95 and availability monitoring, keyboard/screen-reader/WCAG review, deployment/TLS and rollback checks in staging and signed customer/owner UAT.

Known product extensions: supplemental/off-cycle/retro-arrears automation, statutory refund handling, fully automated historical financial backfill and legal deletion/hold workflows. Company logo URLs are displayed in the portals; generated payroll/report headers currently brand with the company name and product attribution. File retention dates do not delete financial/HR records automatically. Report/job outputs have explicit bounded sizes and employee/payslip selectors have current bounded views; large-tenant UX should be validated with representative volumes.

Review the requirement/evidence matrix before closing a launch gate. A rendered screen, local Docker build or synthetic arithmetic case alone is not acceptance evidence.
